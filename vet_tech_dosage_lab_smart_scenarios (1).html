📏 Rounding reference from your vet tech handout

Weights: carry to hundredths, round to tenths.
Capsules: nearest whole capsule.
Tablets: nearest whole; scored tablets may round to nearest half.
mL under 1: nearest hundredth.
1–5 mL: nearest tenth.
Over 5 mL: nearest whole mL.
IV drip rate: nearest whole drop/min.
General: 5 rounds up; include leading zeros and units.

Boundary convention: exactly 1.00 mL is in the 1–5 mL band; exactly 5.00 mL is also in that band. For the purpose of this educational exercise, half-tablet rounding is permitted for scored tablets. Clinical rounding, splitability, appropriate dose, dispensing quantities, and administration must always be verified independently.
